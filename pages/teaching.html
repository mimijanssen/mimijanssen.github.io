I believe that science is strengthened when we engage with and learn from our communities. I resonate with the words of Dr. Rita Colwell, distinguished microbiologist and former director of the National Science Foundation, who wrote in her memoir A Lab of One's Own: “The best 100 percent of the population will always be better than the best of 50 percent of the population.”
